Confirmed Incidents

48+ documented
Accelerating fast. The Institute for Justice had documented 26 cases as of July 2026 — we're now tracking 48+ across 21 states as of Aug 21, 2026. Dallas alone has logged 25+ attacks since March, Minnesota has seen 15+ cities hit since April, and this week (Aug 16-22) is a declared "National Week of Action Against ALPRs." NPR reports vandalism has now been documented in at least 36 states nationwide. Fresh this week: Auburn, NY's council voted unanimously to terminate its Flock contract (Aug 21), Summit County, CO logged 8 cameras cut down across three separate dates (Aug 6-20) out of its 30-camera network, and Allen County/Fort Wayne, IN saw cameras spray-painted blue with an anti-Flock sign quoting Benjamin Franklin left behind (Aug 20). A five-camera spree also hit Adams and Wells County, IN (Aug 18-19, shot, burned, and cut down — the story pulled 3,500+ Facebook comments), a Douglas, MA camera was run over by a vehicle (Aug 17), Gardner, KS declined to renew its Flock contract, and Harrisonburg, VA's system went fully dark after a unanimous council vote. The real number is significantly higher than any tracker can confirm.
Aug 6-20, 2026
Summit County, CO: 8 cameras cut down
A wave of vandalism hit the county's 30-camera ALPR network: four cameras cut down and one repositioned away from the road during the week of Aug 6, then two more cut down Aug 15 (Colorado Highway 9 near Frisco/Breckenridge, Barton Road) and two more Aug 18-20 (Ryan Gulch Road near Wildernest, Cove Blvd at Summit Cove entrance). Sheriff's office says all cases are under investigation as potential felony vandalism. Comes after a 740+ signature petition opposing the county's Flock contracts; a community forum is set for Sept 10.
8 cameras
Jul 13 - Aug 20, 2026
Allen County & Fort Wayne, IN: spray-painted
An Allen County Sheriff's Office camera on Coldwater Road (north of Union Chapel Rd) was spray-painted blue with an anti-Flock sign quoting Benjamin Franklin left in front of it. A second camera northwest of downtown Fort Wayne — one of the city's 36 — was separately vandalized. No arrests; Assistant Chief Ryan Spangler said the damage "causes further delays" in investigations.
2 cameras
Aug 18-19, 2026
Adams & Wells County, IN: shot, burned, cut down
Five ALPR cameras destroyed across two counties in a single week — two in Adams County (solar panel shot, components burned, pole cut down), one in Berne on State Road 218 (cut down), and two more in Wells County. The story drew over 3,500 Facebook comments, many supportive of the vandalism. No arrests; potential felony criminal recklessness charges over the firearm use.
5 cameras
Aug 17, 2026
Douglas, MA: run over by vehicle
A Flock camera on Gilboa Street was knocked roughly 20 feet from its base, pole "flattened to the point of it being adjacent to the ground," solar panel shattered. Tire impressions found 30 feet away point to an intentional hit-and-run. Camera went offline around 9pm; no suspects identified. Town maintains eight Flock cameras total.

Toledo & Oregon, OH: weekend spree
Five Flock cameras cut down or damaged in one weekend across two cities — three in Toledo (I-280 & Starr Ave, Burger & Seaman, 100 block of Burger St), following an earlier camera destroyed near Dorr & Joyce, plus two more in neighboring Oregon, OH.
