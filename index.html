North Suffolk, VA: systematic dismantling
Jeffrey Scott Sovern, 41, USAF engineer, accused of systematically dismantling 13 cameras over 6 months using vice grips. Began by redirecting cameras, escalated to knocking poles down, then throwing cameras off bridges onto interstates. Found with 6 solar panels at home. Faces 19 felony counts + 6 misdemeanors. Pleaded not guilty, argues technology is unconstitutional.
